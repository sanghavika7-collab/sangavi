Understanding the process

What Is Clinical Hypnotherapy?

Clinical hypnotherapy is a guided process that uses focused attention and deep relaxation to help you explore patterns of thinking, feeling and behaviour. In a session, your hypnotherapist may use calming suggestions, imagery and practical strategies tailored to your goals.

Unlike stage hypnosis, clinical hypnotherapy is a collaborative therapeutic experience. You are not asleep or under someone else's control. You can hear, respond and choose whether to follow suggestions throughout the session.

People seek hypnotherapy for habits, stress, fears and emotional wellbeing. It can sit alongside counselling and appropriate medical or psychological treatment. It is not a substitute for diagnosis, prescribed treatment or care from a qualified health professional. If you're exploring clinical hypnotherapy Melbourne, a free consultation is a chance to ask questions and decide whether it feels right for you.

Some policies may provide rebates for eligible treatments or practitioners. Check your fund's current rules and confirm the clinic's participating funds and provider details before booking.

How many sessions will I need?

This varies depending on your concern, goals and response to sessions. Tony can discuss an individual approach and what to expect during the initial conversation.

Do I need a GP referral?

You can enquire about a consultation without first obtaining a GP referral. For ongoing medical or mental health concerns, keep your GP or treating clinician involved in your care.
