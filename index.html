10 / BEFORE YOU BOOK

Everything you need to know before starting your next energy or electrical project.

ASK US A QUESTION ↗

SOLAR & BATTERIES

Can you install solar panels and a home battery together?

Solar panels and home batteries can often be planned as one project. We can review your property, electricity use and preferred system arrangement before quoting.

Can I add a battery to an existing solar system?

It may be possible, depending on your existing inverter, switchboard and system configuration. A site assessment is the best starting point.

ELECTRICAL & SWITCHBOARDS

When might I need a switchboard upgrade?

An upgrade may be needed when your existing switchboard does not meet the requirements of a proposed installation, or when electrical safety issues are identified.

Do you work on both homes and commercial properties?

Yes. We offer domestic and commercial electrical solutions. Contact us with the location and nature of your project to discuss availability.

PRICING & QUOTES

How do I get a quote for electrical or solar work?

Send us your contact details, address or suburb, and the service you need. We can discuss next steps and the information required for a quote.

Does the price depend on the property?

Yes. Pricing depends on the scope of work, equipment, site conditions and any additional electrical upgrades that may be necessary.
