UNDERSTANDING DAY PROGRAMS

What Is an NDIS Day Program?

An NDIS day program provides structured daytime activities for people with disabilities in a welcoming group setting. Activities may take place at a centre, at an accessible community venue or during an outing. The aim is to create opportunities to connect with others, build everyday skills, explore interests and take part in activities that feel meaningful to each person.

These supports are often described as Group and Centre Based Activities. Depending on your goals and funding, ndis group activities may include creative workshops, life skills practice, social activities and supervised community participation.

At Caring Humanity, we work with participants and families to discuss individual interests, support requirements and suitable schedules. Activities and funding arrangements are reviewed before commencement so everyone understands what is included and what may attract an additional cost.

How Day Programs Are Funded Through Your NDIS Plan

NDIS day programs may be funded through Core Supports under Assistance with Social, Economic and Community Participation, where the participant’s plan and funding arrangements allow.

The amount claimed can vary with the agreed support arrangements, including how many staff members support the group, the number of participants and the relevant NDIS pricing rules. Our team can discuss the proposed support ratio and service agreement before your first session.

YOUR QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Frequently Asked Questions

What are NDIS day programs?

They are structured daytime activities designed to support social connection, skill building and community participation. The exact support available depends on the provider and the participant’s individual needs.

Who can join your disability day programs?

These programs are intended for adults with disabilities. Individual eligibility, age requirements and support capacity should be confirmed with Caring Humanity.

What types of activities do you offer?

Examples include creative activities, everyday life skills and opportunities for social connection. The current activity roster should be confirmed before attendance.

Do you provide transport?

Ask our team about collection areas, transport availability, booking arrangements and any applicable charges.

Are meals provided?

Meal arrangements and whether food is included or charged separately need to be confirmed with the program team.

How much does a day program cost under the NDIS?

Costs vary according to the agreed supports, group arrangements, activity costs and relevant NDIS pricing rules. Request an individual quote and service agreement before starting.

What are your staff-to-participant ratios?

The ratio should be discussed based on the activity and each participant’s support requirements. Ask for the ratio proposed for your sessions.

Can you support high or complex needs?

Contact Caring Humanity to discuss specific needs, safety requirements and whether the team has suitable staff and resources.

What should my family member bring?

Ask for a session checklist. This may include personal items, suitable clothing, required medication arrangements and any individual support information.

Can we try a day before committing?

Ask the team whether a trial day is currently offered and whether any fees or eligibility requirements apply.

Do you offer respite as well?

For respite or short-term support, explore Caring Humanity’s separate services and request the correct respite service page link.
