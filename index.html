Welcome to Hendry Pianos

Your Trusted Piano Specialists in Melbourne Since 1970

A passion for piano. A lifetime of experience.

At Hendry Pianos, the piano is more than an instrument. It is an enduring part of our family story. Established by concert organist and composer Michael Hendry Sr. in 1970, our Melbourne business has spent decades guiding pianists, families, educators and performance venues towards instruments they can cherish.

Today, under the direction of Mike Hendry, we combine an experienced, personal approach with specialist knowledge of acoustic piano sales, tuning, repairs and restoration. Whether you are choosing your first piano or caring for a treasured instrument, we take the time to understand what matters to you.
